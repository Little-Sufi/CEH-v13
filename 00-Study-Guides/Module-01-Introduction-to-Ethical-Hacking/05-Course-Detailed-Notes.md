## 📜 1. Cyber Law & Compliance Frameworks

- **Computer Fraud and Abuse Act (CFAA)**: Primary US federal statute prohibiting unauthorized computer access and fraud.
- **General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR)**: EU data privacy framework regulating personal data processing and storage.
- **HIPAA**: US health data protection act safeguarding Protected Health Information (PHI).
- **PCI-DSS**: Payment Card Industry Data Security Standard governing credit card transaction security.
