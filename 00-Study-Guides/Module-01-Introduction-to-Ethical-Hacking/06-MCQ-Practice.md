### 🧩 Question 01 | Scenario Challenge

> [!QUESTION] 📌 **Scenario Context**
> A financial software application calculates annual loss expectations for an enterprise data center. The asset value (AV) of the primary database server cluster is $1,000,000. Risk assessment indicates that a major database corruption incident has an exposure factor (EF) of 0.40 (40% loss of value) and an Annualized Rate of Occurrence (ARO) of 0.25 (once every 4 years).
>
> What is the calculated Annualized Loss Expectancy (ALE) for this risk scenario?

#### 🔘 Available Options:

- **A**: $100,000
- **B**: $400,000
- **C**: $250,000
- **D**: $1,000,000

> [!SUCCESS]- 🔑 **Show Correct Answer & Exam Breakdown**
> **Correct Answer**: **Option A**
>
> #### 💡 Technical Deep Dive:
> Risk calculations follow two standard equations:
> 1. Single Loss Expectancy ($SLE$) = $Asset\ Value\ (AV) \times Exposure\ Factor\ (EF) = \$1,000,000 \times 0.40 = \$400,000$.
> 2. Annualized Loss Expectancy ($ALE$) = $SLE \times ARO = \$400,000 \times 0.25 = \$100,000$.
>
> #### 🚫 Distractor Analysis & Refutations:
>
> - **Option B is incorrect** because $400,000 represents the Single Loss Expectancy (SLE), not the Annualized Loss Expectancy (ALE).
> - **Option C is incorrect** because $250,000 reflects multiplying AV by ARO without accounting for EF.
> - **Option D is incorrect** because $1,000,000 is the total Asset Value (AV).
