## 📌 1. SCADA, DCS & PLC Architectural Differences

- **Programmable Logic Controller (PLC)**: Ruggedized industrial computer executing real-time control loops based on sensor inputs and ladder logic.
- **SCADA (Supervisory Control and Data Acquisition)**: Centrally monitors and controls geographically dispersed industrial assets over wide areas.
- **DCS (Distributed Control System)**: Controls plant-centric industrial processes locally within a single manufacturing facility.

> [!INFO] 📋 **Comprehensive Industrial Control Hardware Comparison Matrix**

| Control Hardware | Geographic Scope | Control Mechanism | Primary Application |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **PLC** | Local Sub-system | Direct real-time I/O control loop | Manufacturing line, valve control |
| **SCADA** | Geographically Dispersed | High-level supervisory monitoring | Power grid distribution, oil pipelines |
| **DCS** | Single Plant Facility | Integrated distributed control | Chemical refineries, paper mills |
