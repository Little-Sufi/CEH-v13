---


---


---


---

## 🎯 Objective
Cloning a sample portal to audit credential transmission.

## 📝 Commands
In the website attack vectors menu:
- Select option `3` (Credential Harvester Attack Method)
- Select option `2` (Site Cloner)
- Enter your local IP address for the POST back action.
- Enter the URL to clone (e.g. `http://example.com/login`).
